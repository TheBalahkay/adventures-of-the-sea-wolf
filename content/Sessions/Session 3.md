Here at the docks of [[Rocktide Cove]], the crew of [[The Sea Wolf]], fought off a surprise Sahuagin attack. After the battle they found a hefty amount of gold, magical items, and gems at the bottom of the bay. The people of [[Rocktide Cove]] were so grateful that they invited the group to set up a base of operations there.
