The crew of [[The Sea Wolf]] found themselves at the top of a mountain in the range surrounding [[Krestis]]. They made their way down, until coming across a large building which seemed to be associated with the [[Ashen Wing]], much to Pippin's dismay. They spent a long long long time forming a plan to fight the gang: Everyone had extra hp and they had [[Huckleberry]] and [[Hardough]] up front bursting through the doors right as [[Pippin Tangletoes]] flew by with a pair of skis. The rest of the party was ready towards the rear to help as soon as possible. When they burst in they couldn't immediately tell that it was just a bar, [[The Mace and Flail]]. [[Huckleberry]] and [[Newt]] did accidentally kill two dwarves, and [[Pippin Tangletoes]] ended up in the middle of a fighting pit (and won!).

They then did some fights and gathered info.
