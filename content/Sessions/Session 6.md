[[Lok'tar]] began this session with a fight against a [[Displacer Beast]]. After besting the beast, [[Gaius]] indicated that he wanted [[Lok'tar]] to finish the beast off, but instead he feigned a finishing move and told the animal to play dead. [[Lok'tar]] then snuck the [[Displacer Beast]] into his room and brought it the dead dwarves that [[Huckleberry]] and [[Newt]] (accidentally) killed the previous day for it to feed on, in order to tame the beast. The group convened in his room to have a finally chat about their lives when [[Pike Steer]], a worker at the [[Ashen Wing Fighting Outpost]], suspiciously opened the door and glanced in the room and quickly ran away. [[Casstus Wolfe]], [[Huckleberry]], and [[Lok'tar]] pursued him to his room and busted in his door (which they replaced with one from an empty room). They intimidated [[Pike Steer]], who then revealed his affiliation with the [[Fire Claws]], and their plan to usurp the [[Ashen Wing]] gang. [[Pike Steer]] had been gathering intelligence about the [[Ashen Wing Fighting Outpost]], and had even dug a hole from his room into the first lower level. He also agreed that if the crew of [[The Sea Wolf]], were to help in these efforts they could share in the profits and have any items that they wanted to collect from the establishment. [[Pike Steer]] also wanted to use the [[Displacer Beast]] as a distraction while they fight and kill [[Gaius]], [[Morton]], and any other [[Ashen Wing]] higher-ups.

After discussing in private— the group came to the conclusion that, at the very least, letting these two groups fight would weaken them, and leave an opportunity for later. [[Huckleberry]] left [[Pike Steer]] pinned under his immovable rod and drunk from the three tankards of ale [[Lok'tar]] forced down his throat. 

[[Lok'tar]] and [[Casstus Wolfe]] also gleaned from the barkeep information about the [[Berserker]], and [[Frost Worms]].
