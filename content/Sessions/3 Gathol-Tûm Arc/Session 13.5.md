The crew of [[The Sea Wolf]] delve deeper into the caverns. After the chamber where [[Gus Gus]] resides they came across a familiar sight, a magical fog of darkness like they had seen in [[Shar]]'s domain below  [[The Mace and Flail]]. Leading into the darkness was only a rickety wooden bridge.

The crew proceeded with extreme caution. Slowly trying out several methods to probe the darkness. [[Dwayne]] bravely agreed to be tied to a rope and swung into the darkness. Next, [[Newt]] agreed to be the first of the crew to brave it herself. Once inside she realized that it was not only sight, but also sound, that was dampened. 

Everyone tied themselves together and made their way across. Someone discovered an odd crystal alongside the bridge—and once it was destroyed, some darkness abated. When they could hear again, they heard someone moaning further along the bridge. [[Newt]] in the lead destroyed another crystal (pocketing some) and saw an undead person standing on a ledge, moaning.

When they examined the docile creature, it looked as if he was wearing some sort of fine cloak. It wasn't actually a cloak, but a [[Cloaker]].

It's flight capability made it a huge pain to deal with, and at one point it started sucking on [[Echo Dawn Silvertail of Feather-Hoot| Echo]]'s head like a binky. She was able to put the creature to sleep with her magic, and carefully remove her head without waking it. At that point the whole party struck it with all their might and defeated the beast.

They then continued on in search of the [[Wyrm (Gusu Katakata)]].
