The crew of the [[The Sea Wolf]] continue their fight against [[Wyrm (Gusu Katakata)]]. Early on in the second half of the battle, Gusu caused the magma to rise which separated the group. However the group manages to land several nice blows, and the wyrm asked to parler. Gusu offered to let them all go, and take his treasure under the condition that they help to kill [[High King Tenzu IV]]. Gusu revealed that he was not from this plane and wishes to go home. The magic that King Tenzu used to protect [[Chandri]], also sealed him here.

While [[Echo Dawn Silvertail of Feather-Hoot|Echo]] was somewhat sympathetic to his cause, [[Castus Wolfe]] was completely against going against the High King. In the end, the group decided that IF they ended up going against the high king, they would do so of their own volition. And because Gusu could not commit to a began lifestyle, they continued to fight. 

It may have been at this point, or later during the fight but [[Castus Wolfe]] was temporarily transported to talk with a lawyer of [[Tiamut]]. Apparently he was offered a [[Wyrmlings|Red Dragon Wyrmling]] if he could spare Gusu, and the group would have to deal with the wrath of [[Tiamut]] if they slayed Gusu. He made his case to the others, but they were suspicious of him. He decided against making a deal with the devil.

It was an arduous task still ahead of them. At several points, many of the crew dropped hitpoints. [[Oryx Valeshade]] managed to shoot an arrow attached to some rope out of the caldera in case they needed to escape. [[Newt]] prayed to [[Selûne]] to help her in battle and was answered in a glorious fashion with a weapon that glowed like the moon. [[Huckleberry]] hurriedly scooped up all the loot that he could fit into the bag of holding. Eventually, someone struck the killing blow.

In the end they were able to take the dragons hoard, and several valuable bits from Gusu, and the [[Dragon]] card.

[[Castus Wolfe]] took the card, and he also switched his [[Key]] card for [[Oryx Valeshade]]'s [[Sage]] card. The crew also recharged their cards in the wyrm's magical blood.
