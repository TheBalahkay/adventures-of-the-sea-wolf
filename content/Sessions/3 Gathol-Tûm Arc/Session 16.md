After defeating [[Wyrm (Gusu Katakata)|Gusu Katakata]] the group had to decide whether to leave out the top of [[Krestis]], or to return to [[Gathol~Tûm]]. [[Oryx Valeshade]] climbed the rope to scout out the volcano, but found nothing. They then decided that it would be best to return to [[Gathol~Tûm]], and make their way to the ship.

While walking back, [[Huckleberry]]'s arm started to cause him serious pain. To the point where he almost had someone cut it off, but he decided to wait.

As they passed by the area where they found [[Gus Gus]], the group found a transportation circle. Since blindly jumping into things had always worked out for them, they went in and found [[Woodrot]]. They then made some purchases from [[Gus Gus]] and after selling him some items they got some spores that would create their own transportation circle to [[Woodrot]]. [[Gus Gus]] also told them that [[Huckleberry]]'s would need a strong remove curse spell in order to heal.

[[Newt]] thought that she could help, so she took [[Huckleberry]] into a corner, carved a [[Selûne]] symbol into the wall and started to pray. Through her healing efforts and prayer, she learned that there were actually several things going on inside his arm. Some sort of mixture of curse and physical ailment was rotting it from the inside out— and worse, it was endangering the [[World Tree]]. [[Huckleberry]] initially asked her to cut it off right then and there, but [[Newt]] thought he had some time. He made her swear that if it got any worse, she would do whatever it took to save the [[World Tree]], be it cutting off his arm, or killing him outright. 

Before leaving, [[Gus Gus]] made a comment about how they should explore the tomb underneath the citadel of [[High King Tenzu IV]].

After some more walking they made it back to [[Gathol~Tûm]]. They gave a report to [[Foreman Blevitt]] that the direction they went was fruitless AND haunted, so they should avoid it at all cost.

[[Oryx Valeshade]] went off to find an escape route. He was successful. All it took was convincing two bored guards that he was a maintenance man, and he then had access to a vent. He made it out and to  [[The Sea Wolf]], before returning.

The group hatched an escape plan which included smashing and grabbing the [[Gem]] card, when [[Breem D'Brov]] came around and said that after spending so much time there they were trustworthy and could leave. He also tasked them with delivering some weapons and other materials to a resistance outpost at [[Smeltmore]].

There was now a new plan, most of the group would take the materials out the main entrance while someone would get the gem (and maybe [[Magnyl]] if he was willing). They went to go speak with [[Magnyl]] who was hesitant at first. When [[Echo Dawn Silvertail of Feather-Hoot|Echo]] reached for the [[Gem]] card it went directly to [[Magnyl]]. Then [[Huckleberry]] showed him that they have more cards, and tried to make a demonstration about how they worked but accidentally switched cards (but quickly switched back). [[Magnyl]] knew he could do more research with them, he agreed to go.

Echo took him through a dimension door, while the others walked out. Once all aboard the boat, it became clear that [[Gathol~Tûm]] knew what had happened. They made several attempts to shoot the [[The Sea Wolf]] with their cannons, but nothing hit. They sailed away.
