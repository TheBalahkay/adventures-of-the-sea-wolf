The crew of [[The Sea Wolf]] decide come across the remains of a defeated adventuring party. From the loot that they find, specifically a scroll requiring a high level, they determine that they may not be quite ready to face [[Wyrm (Gusu Katakata)]]. With the intent of returning soon they decide to head back for [[Gathol~Tûm]]. Upon their arrival they ask around about [[Wyrm (Gusu Katakata)]], [[Breem D'Brov]], and [[Foreman Blevitt]] offer what little they know. They also use the materials that they had gathered on their adventures and in the mines to acquire some new loot from [[Magnyl]]. They sell the rest in hopes that it will be enough (combined with whatever treasure they get from [[Wyrm (Gusu Katakata)]]) will be enough to fund a new ship. [[Newt]] also spent her time learning the spell from the spell scroll.

They then make their way back down to face the wyrm. When they finally enter the caldera of the beast, they can make out a fully grown red wyrm sleeping on a tower. After creating a brief plan each of the party members take up a position behind cover. Their goal was to spread out and land their first round of attacks at the same time. 

As [[Huckleberry]] dropped from the ceiling towards the wyrm (safely thanks to [[Echo Dawn Silvertail of Feather-Hoot]]'s feather fall spell), [[Oryx Valeshade]] took a shot at it's eye with his [[Oathbow]]. The battle commenced. They had several controlled rounds of combat in which they landed several hits, forced the wyrm to burn through it's legendary resistances, and stayed safe from it's fiery breath. Luckily the mushrooms that [[Newt]] and [[Huckleberry]] had hastily eaten from [[Gus Gus]] had given them resistance to fire damage.

At one point [[Newt]] was hit onto a stream of lava, but was able to land herself on a cool bit of floating rock. At the next opportunity, she scrambled off and found cover.

[[Castus Wolfe]], [[Echo Dawn Silvertail of Feather-Hoot|Echo]], and [[Pippin Tangletoes]] all made effective attacks— but [[Oryx Valeshade]] was pumping out damage. [[Huckleberry]] abandoned his plan to grapple the wyrm in his huge form and instead made attacks with the [[Sword of Pity]]. At some point he realized that he should have been making more effective attacks this whole time and in a burst of rage (and embarrassment) let loose an attack that made up for all that he had missed. In the end they depleted the wyrm's health below half and had only a few members who had taken severe damage.
