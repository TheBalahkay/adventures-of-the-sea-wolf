The party awoke in  [[The Mace and Flail]] and let [[Pike Steer]] go his way. [[Gaius]] came to the suite and invited the group to the exhibition between [[Berserker]] and an unknown creature in two days.  As they prepared to depart, they acquired [[The Left Finger test]], two [[Hooked Polearm]], and a few bottles of [[Special Health Potion]].

Descending from the mountain path, they reached the small port town below. There, [[Huckleberry]] reclaimed his anchor from the blacksmith, while [[Pippin Tangletoes]] commissioned leather armor for [[Lady Cluckington]]. The blacksmith spoke in hushed tones of the **Ashen Wing**, a ruthless group that extracted heavy tolls under the guise of “protection” from wild creature attacks — little more than a shakedown in disguise.

Determined to continue their journey, the party climbed back up the mountainside and bonded over sharing about their history. [[Lok'tar]] stayed with the [[Displacer Beast]], and the rest reentered the **mysterious cave** where they had once been transported. Winding through its tunnels, they emerged into a vast, echoing cavern — and there, the ground began to tremble.

A [[Remorhaz]] erupted from the stone. Battle was joined. 

The group threw out some torches and split into two: [[Pippin Tangletoes]], [[Newt]], and [[Huckleberry]] circled to the right— [[Oryx Valeshade]], [[Casstus Wolfe]], and [[Hardough]] went left. [[Casstus Wolfe]] hit first with his silent vicious mockery, [[Hardough]] and [[Pippin Tangletoes]] [[Newt]] and [[Huckleberry]] all made attacks that did next to nothing.

[[Oryx Valeshade]] then hit a crit and hooked the beast with _The Left Finger_, but was soon swallowed whole — only to strike back from within its molten core, damaging the creature from the inside. [[Huckleberry]] used his decanter of endless water to try to douse the [[Remorhaz]], but it just creates a cloud of steam around it, glowing bright orange in the darkness. [[Pippin Tangletoes]] uses his action and action surge to command [[Oryx Valeshade]] to attack twice from the inside where he rolled another crit! All the while taking _6d6_ damage from the heat, he gets down to 8 hp. [[Newt]], thinking fast, dove into the mouth of the [[Remorhaz]] and poured a health potion down to Oryx’s fiery prison. [[Oryx Valeshade]], struggled against the [[Remorhaz]], and managed to receive most of the potion, but got drunk in the process. [[Casstus Wolfe]] did more damage from vicious mockery, [[Pippin Tangletoes]] commanded [[Newt]] to attack from the inside now (instead of causing [[Oryx Valeshade]] to do it and take more damage) and she lands the finishing blow. The plan worked, and the great beast fell.

Before the group could celebrate, the dying [[Remorhaz]]'s struggle caused stalactites to fall. The stalactites fell and scraped [[Newt]]/ [[Lady Cluckington]], but fell directly onto [[Oryx Valeshade]]— downing him. [[Casstus Wolfe]] gave his [[Special Health Potion]] to [[Newt]] who avoided intoxication, and [[Pippin Tangletoes]] quickly retrieved an extra bottle from [[Lady Cluckington]], and poured it down [[Oryx Valeshade]] mouth and revives him— luckily, [[Oryx Valeshade]] succeeded against becoming blackout drunk from his second potion of the night.

Suddenly, a young [[Remorhaz]] burst forth. The group made a round of strong hits against it, and [[Huckleberry]] hurled his **Javelin of Lightning**, smiting the creature and causing it try to burrow away. [[Pippin Tangletoes]] and [[Lady Cluckington]] gave chase, striking the killing blow in a flurry of feathers and fury.

When the dust settled, the heroes harvested the [[Remorhaz]] [[Chitin]] for future crafting — trophies of their victory — and felt a surge of strength and experience course through them.

**The party leveled up.**
