In the aftermath of the final clash at [[The Mace and Flail]], thr group gained three more cards from the [[Deck of Many Things]]: **Monstosity**, **Construct** and **Fang** (which [[Oryx Valeshade]] is holding on to and will respawn the Naga in fours days. The group started by familiarizing themselves more with [[Echo Dawn Silvertail of Feather-Hoot]], who asked how the group got to where they are now, [[Castus Wolfe]] relayed that during the battle he briefly visited space, grâce à [[High Priestess Nyx]], and saw a comet hurling toward [[Chandri]].

The group milled about, looking at the chasm for awhile before [[Huckleberry]] remembered the creatures in cages above. He threw a magical cage with a fire elemental down the chasm, hoping to see it reach the bottom. Instead, he found that it took ~5 seconds until the magic in the cage dispelled, and another ~20 seconds until the light of the elemental could no longer be seen. The chasm turned out to be [[Shar]]'s domain.

The group killed two cursed scholars [[Nohics]] , and took their eyes before finding some young [[Wyrmlings]]. Someone could tell that they were starving, so Echo cooked the eyes in some bear fat and fed the [[Wyrmlings]] before the group opened the cage. Once released, flying beasts did not attack the group, but did start to incinerate all the other evil creatures. The group decided to save only the [[Flail Snails]] (X6). The only creature that didn't die to the flames was a cambion, who screeched in pain as the [[Wyrmlings]] refused to relent. In pity several of the members of [[The Sea Wolf]] attacked to put it out of it's misery. [[Oryx Valeshade]] and [[Huckleberry]] struck the killing blows and were cursed momentarily before [[Newt]] stepped up to heal them. With nothing left to eat, the [[Wyrmlings]] flew away, down the dark chasm. [[Castus Wolfe]] said a quick prayer to [[Bahamut]].

Upon reaching the ground floor, which reeked of unspeakable odors, [[Castus Wolfe]] noticed that [[Splint]] and the barmaid were despondent. He offered for them to take up residence at [[Rocktide Cove]], in exchange for armor and they agreed.

Suddenly a courier ran in. He presented himself as a servant of [[Baron Agnon]] and delivered several messages and pieces of mail. He then started writing an account of what transpired, and attempted to make a painting of the group. [[Newt]] snatched his drawing supplies and ran away with it so that he could not capture their likeness (Huckleberry was a little disappointed as he had never been drawn before). 

Letters Received:
- [[Huckleberry]] received a letter from [[Belladonna]], letting him know the [[The Sea Wolf]] has been upgraded and she wishes to be the first mate

- [[Castus Wolfe]] received a letter from his wife, [[Camille Wolfe]], with updates of his family. One of his younger brothers has fallen in with a bad crowd and [[Castus' Children]] are doing okay, expect for the youngest, Arryn, who has fallen ill with a mysterious illness. His older brother, [[Leonardo Wolfe]], is dealing with the [[Chandri/Minor Events/Panthia Rebellion]].

- [[Oryx Valeshade]] also received a letter, which he secretly read.

- [[Echo Dawn Silvertail of Feather-Hoot]] received a note from [[Mokato]] asking about updates and about an upcoming meeting with all Deans at the [[Imperial Arcane Academy]]

- [[Newt]] did not receive a letter

- Mysterious Group Letter: One of the letters that the group received was unintelligible at first glace but it turns out that it was written partly in orcish. Although it took some deciphering, it turned out that it was from some sort of secret society that had 'been watching us' and 'will be seeing us soon'. The message then poofed into GP 200.

The courier begins investigating the rest of the building to finish his write-up and eventually leaves to report back to [[Baron Agnon]]. 

[[Newt]] sees the moon outside one of the windows and rushed outside to feel the fresh air and commune with the moon in a religious dance. [[Echo Dawn Silvertail of Feather-Hoot]] is intrigued by this cultural phenomenon and follows Newt out. She ends up poorly dancing with feathers flying around, compared to Newt's rehearsed and elegant footwork. [[Castus Wolfe]] plays a mesmerizing vocal gig. After some dancing and if in answer to [[Newt]]'s prayer the moon's phase shifted from waxing gibbous to first quarter and she was blessed by [[Selûne]]. Meanwhile, [[Oryx Valeshade]] remained indoors to set some simple traps and then joined [[Huckleberry]] and [[Pippin Tangletoes]] to go loot [[Gaius]]'s room. There were several pieces of treasure that everyone in the group found there.

Feeling good about leaving [[The Mace and Flail]] to the reinforcements from [[House Wolfe]], the group then descended the mountain and finally re-boarded [[The Sea Wolf]]. After some discussion, they decided to sail to [[Rocktide Cove]] before finding the home of the [[Imprisoned Dwarves]].

On the passage [[Belladonna]] told them about some ships/ship upgrades available in [[Coral Bay]] and about a bounty for an [[Aboleth]] nearby (GP 2,000). [[Castus Wolfe]] gave a brooch he found from [[Gaius]]'s room to the dwarves who marked a new location on our map!
