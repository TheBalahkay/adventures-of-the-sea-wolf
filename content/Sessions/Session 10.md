Moon Phase: Waxing Gibbous --> First Quarter

In the aftermath of the final clash at [[The Mace and Flail]], the group started by familiarizing themselves more with [[Echo Dawn Silvertail of Feather-Hoot]]. [[Castus Wolfe]] relayed that during the battle he briefly visited space, grâce à [[High Priestess Nyx]], and saw a comet hurling toward [[Chandri]]. 

The group milled about, looking at the chasm for awhile before [[Huckleberry]] remembered the creatures in cages above. He threw a magical cage with a fire elemental down the chasm, hoping to see it reach the bottom. Instead, he found that it took ~5 seconds until the magic in the cage dispelled, and another ~20 seconds until the light of the elemental could no longer be seen.

The group killed two cursed scholars, and took their eyes before finding some young [[Wyrmlings]]. Someone could tell that they were starving, so Echo cooked the eyes in some bear fat and fed the [[Wyrmlings]] before the group opened the cage. Once released, flying beasts did not attack the group, but did start to incinerate all the other evil creatures. The group decided to save only the flail snails (X6). The only creature that didn't die to the flames was a cambion, who screeched in pain as the [[Wyrmlings]] refused to relent. In pity several of the members of [[The Sea Wolf]] attacked to put it out of it's misery. [[Oryx Valeshade]] and [[Huckleberry]] struck the killing blows and were cursed momentarily before [[Newt]] stepped up to heal them. With nothing left to eat, the [[Wyrmlings]] flew away, down the dark chasm. Castus said a quick prayer to [[Bahamut]].

Upon reaching the ground floor, Castus noticed that [[Splint]] and the barmaid were despondent. He offered for them to take up residence at [[Rocktide Cove]] and they agreed.

Suddenly a courier ran in. He presented himself as a servant of [[Baron Agnon]] and delivered several messages and pieces of mail. He then started writing an account of what transpired, and attempted to make a drawing of the group. [[Newt]] snatched his drawing supplies and ran away with it so that he could not capture their likeness (Huckleberry was a little disappointed as he had never been drawn before).

One of the letters that the group received was unintelligible at first glace but it turns out that it was written partly in orcish. Although it took some deciphering, it turned out that it was from some sort of secret society that had 'been watching us' and 'will be seeing us soon'. The message then poofed into GP 200.

Newt rushed outside to feel the fresh air and commune with the moon, and several people followed her. They decided to dance in the moonlight— and as if in answer to her prayer the moon's phase shifted from waxing gibbous to first quarter. Oryx remained indoors to set some simple traps and then joined Huck to go loot [[Gaius]]'s room. There were several pieces of treasure that everyone in the group found there.

Feeling good about leaving [[The Mace and Flail]] to the reinforcements from [[House Wolfe]], the group then descended the mountain and finally re-boarded  [[The Sea Wolf]]. After some discussion, they decided to sail to [[Rocktide Cove]] before finding the home of the [[Imprisoned Dwarves]]. On the passage [[Belladonna]] told them about some ships/ship upgrades available in [[Coral Bay]] and about a bounty for an [[Aboleth]] nearby (GP 2,000).
