The crew of [[The Sea Wolf]] mess up an [[Aboleth]]. 

Notable moments: 

[[Pippin Tangletoes]] and [[Newt]] are used as bait to draw out the creature from the deep.

[[Echo Dawn Silvertail of Feather-Hoot]] strikes first using her magic missiles.

[[Castus Wolfe]] created a cloud of daggers to great success.

[[Oryx Valeshade]] is charmed and turns on [[Huckleberry]]. After hitting him with the [[Oathbow]], they temporarily became sworn enemies.

[[Huckleberry]] is charmed and turns on [[Pippin Tangletoes]] and [[Newt]]. He threw them off the ship, and then tried to direct the crew to leave.

The [[Aboleth]] asks for mercy, and promises treasure in return. The group does not relent. They kill the creature.
