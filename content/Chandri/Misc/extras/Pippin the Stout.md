Written about Pippin after an anvil fell from the upper floors of a tavern and landed on his head. Pippin was 18 at the time.

--Verse--
In the corner sat a halfling stout
With a grin so wide
the gods turned out
Pippin Tangle-Toes
The tale unfolds
With a mug in hand and a heart of gold

--Chorus--
An anvil fell
Two stories high
The crowd screamed loud
Thought he’d surely die
But Pippin stood
With his ale in tow
“Another round for all
Let the spirits flow!”

--Verse 2--
The barkeep stared
His jaw agape
As Pippin laughed
Not a scratch on his nape
“I’ve been through worse
now pour me one more"
And now we make his story heard
