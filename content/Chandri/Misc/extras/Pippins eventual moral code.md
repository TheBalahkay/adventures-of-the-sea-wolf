## Pippin's Grounding Principles

- **Priority:** The **free will** of all creatures (people, beasts, monstrosities) is the highest moral good.

- **Cardinal Sin:** **Trampling a creature's free will without due cause** is unnacceptable.

- **Mandate:** Pippin must act to **prevent the violation of free will** (tyranny, trafficking, unnecessary suffering) by others.
----
### How these principles play out

- **Honor:** There is **no honor** in defeating a creature that has no chance against you; skill must be used for necessary defense, not cruelty or pride.

- **Acknowledge the Cost:** After a kill, take a moment to silently ask:  "Was this kill necessary? Let the price of this life strengthen my shield against those who would steal freedom. Wish their spirit well."

- **Dedicate the Resource/Safety:** Vow to use anything gained (loot, survival, safety) _only_ to **further the mission of protecting freedom**, thereby honoring the life that was sacrificed. Do this even when eating a previously living creature.

- **Ritual of Atonement:** If the kill was dishonorable, reflect on it. Dedicate the tainted resource (like the Remorhaz shield) to specific acts of **service and defense of the truly defenseless**. Do your best to honor the death with renewed commitment to your principles.

- **Defining Due Cause:** It is defined as **necessary preventative action** (self-defense or stopping a greater harm/tyranny), not punishment or convenience. If a non-lethal option exists, it must be taken.
	- **Exception:** Individuals who violate free will (like Gaius) and are judged **untrustworthy** to stop/attempt to atone, making their execution a necessary **preventative measure** for the future freedom of others, not just revenge for the past.

- **Dignity in Death:** If a kill is necessary, it must be **swift, clean, and never prolonged for the sake of inflicting suffering. The defeated must be allowed a final, small act of free will (like last words).

### Regarding Life, Resources, and Honor

- **The Circle of Life:** Killing animals for **essential survival** (food) is an acceptance of the natural order and is justified, provided the resources are not wasted.

- **Monstrosities:** Monstrosities are given consideration, but their corrupted or inherently destructive nature means neutralizing them often meets the threshold of **"due cause"** if they threaten safety.

- **Honoring the Kill:** The life taken must be honored with a ritual small personal ritual at pippins earliest availability. This involves **dedicating the strength/resources gained** _only_ to the pursuit of defending free will, thereby ensuring the life was not wasted. If he can he should do this at the site of the kill.

### The Remorhaz Incident: An example

- **Justified Kill, Dishonorable Act:** The Remorhaz kills were strategically justified (resources/threat neutralization), but the **pursuit of the fleeing one** was an act of pride and dishonorable.

- **Pippins Satisfaction:** Feeling satisfaction in **skill and competence** is acceptable; feeling satisfaction in **cruelty or overpowering the defenseless** is a betrayal of his principles.

- **Amends and the Shield:** Pippin must treat the Remorhaz shield as a **moral burden** and a physical reminder that he must strive to do better, even if he fails. Its use is acceptable, but it must be perpetually dedicated to defending the most vulnerable.
