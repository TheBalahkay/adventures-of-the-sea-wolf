Recent events have caused Pippin to start struggling with his morality. He is
feeling extreme internal conflict right now. I have answers to these questions
mostly worked out. But Pippin in game does not.

Currently Pippin is struggling with the desire for vengeance, his principle to honor
free will, and his killing of not just the remorhaz but one that was running away. He
is upset with himself because he let his pride and desire for glory be the driving
force behind killing the second remorhaz. He has only ever trained with OMF. His
moral compass has not been truly tested yet.

On top of that he has been dreaming up methods of torturing gaius for revenge as
payment for how he treated the villagers and specifically the children.


Questions he is asking himself:
- Does he really act as though he values free will? He thinks he does but he has done things recently that is making him question himself
- What justifies the taking of a creatures free will?
- Torturing gaius is probably immoral, but he wants to make him suffer fort he pain he has caused. How does he balance this with his principle of not causing undue suffering?
- He doesn’t eat eggs, but he does eat animals. How is this ok?
- Are monstrosities worthy of the same moral consideration that beasts are? What is the line?
- Was killing the remorhaz even ok at all? They were technically minding their own business. Why should he be ok with killing them?
- How does he properly honor those he has killed?
- Was his satisfaction and pride in killing the remorhaz ok?
