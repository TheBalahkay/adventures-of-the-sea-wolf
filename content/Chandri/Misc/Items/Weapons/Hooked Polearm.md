### Overview
- Damage: 1d6
- Reach: 10ft
- Special Properties:
	- on hit creature is grappled 
		- DC 8 + Proficiency Bonus + Strength Modifier

### Description:

> This pole-arm has a reach of 10ft, deals 1d6 piercing damage, the heavy and two-handed properties, and one additional property. When the wielder hits a creature with an attack with this weapon, it is grappled with the weapon (escape DC 8 + Proficiency Bonus + Strength Modifier).
