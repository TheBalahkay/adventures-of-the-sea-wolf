### Overview
- Damage: 1d10 slashing
- Reach: 5ft
- Requirements: Strength 14, Dexterity 14
- Properties: Heavy, Two-Handed
- Special Properties:
	- A creature hit by this weapon cannot make opportunity attacks against the wielder until the wielder's next turn

### Description:

> This greatsword features a forward-curving blade that rewards momentum and agile footwork. While it hits as hard as other greatswords, it demands both strength and dexterity from its wielder. When the wielder connects with a strike, the weapon's sweeping arc forces the target to overextend, preventing it from taking opportunity attacks against the wielder until the wielder's next turn.
