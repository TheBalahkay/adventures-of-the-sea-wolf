### Overview
- Armor Class Bonus: Provides 3/4 cover (+5 AC and Dexterity saving throws) from one chosen direction
- Requirements: Strength 17
- Properties: Shield, Heavy
- Special Properties:
	- Directional cover is chosen during the wielder's turn and maintained until the next turn
	- The wielder can use their reaction to change the covered direction
	- Wielders have disadvantage on Stealth and Acrobatics checks

### Description:

> These oversized shields, commonly called greatshields or tower shields, are crafted to turn their bearers into moving bulwarks. When braced in a chosen direction, the shield forms a wall that wards off incoming blows and projectiles, providing exceptional protection at the cost of agility. Each round the wielder selects a facing to protect, and with practiced maneuvering can shift that facing in response to new threats. The sheer mass and surface area, however, hinder stealthy movement or acrobatic dodges.
