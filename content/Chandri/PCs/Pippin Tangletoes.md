Pippin is a halfling fighter from the [[Isles of Elysia]]. Some time ago, he stole the egg containing [[Lady Cluckington]], from the [[Ashen Wing]] gang. He was then trained by his mentor [[Old Man Feathers]] on how to train Cluckington as a mount. Pippin has a strong interest in birds, and other creatures. He is a currently traveling as a part of the crew of [[The Sea Wolf]], and a holder of a card from the [[Deck of Many Things]].
