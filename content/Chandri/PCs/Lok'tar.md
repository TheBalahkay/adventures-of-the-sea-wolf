Lok'tar is a bugbear druid from the [[Isles of Elysia]]. He is a companion to his wolf, [[Dennis]]. He is friendly and always ready for hijinks, but is very protective over creatures and living things. He is currently traveling as crew of [[The Sea Wolf]].  He is currently trying to gain the confidence of a [[Displacer Beast]] at the [[The Mace and Flail]]. Lok'tar is a holder of a card from the [[Deck of Many Things]].

**

Loktar is the youngest of 12 siblings, all of whom were killed or eaten by his parents and due to the trauma of seeing this he hates the idea of hurting anyone regardless of his bugbear nature. He has been traveling as a nomad since escaping his family, tending to the animals in the forest and learning their behaviors and befriending many of them. Most the ones he befriended he has saved when finding them injured, since most of them are afraid of his appearance at first. He is intrigued by magic tricks, gold, storytelling, and people watching, all of which makes him really bad at his people skills.



Can speak: Orc, Arracockra, Common, Goblin, Giant, Primordial, Abyssal, Sylvan ....
