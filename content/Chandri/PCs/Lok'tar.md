Lok'tar is a bugbear druid from the [[Isles of Elysia]]. He is a companion to his wolf, [[Dennis]]. He is friendly and always ready for hijinks, but is very protective over creatures and living things. He is currently traveling as crew of [[The Sea Wolf]].  He is currently trying to gain the confidence of a [[Displacer Beast]] at the [[Ashen Wing Fighting Outpost]]. Lok'tar is a holder of a card from the [[Deck of Many Things]].
