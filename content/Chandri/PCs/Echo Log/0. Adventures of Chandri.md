Echo is still learning about the world around her. She takes notes about what she sees, tastes, hears, and feels. She decided to catalogue her adventures with the crew of [[The Sea Wolf]], as one of her quirks is to compulsively collect and archive information.

[[Mokato]] has sent her to find the crew of the [[The Sea Wolf]] to aid them in stopping the [[Cult of the Comet]]. She's excited to learn more about the crew, the cult and the world around her, being secluded to [[Imperial Arcane Academy]] and the [[The Emerald Vale]] for most of her life.
