Casstus is a High-Elf Bard, and member of the noble [[House Wolfe]]. Born as a noble and middle son (two older brothers and one younger?), he was a recipient of a card from the [[Deck of Many Things]].

After the mutiny at sea, Casstus sneakily convinced the crew to rename the ship "[[The Sea Wolf]]" and to fly a flag very similar to that of his house. Casstus' motivation seems to be to advance the goals of [[House Wolfe]] (and thereby [[High King Tenzu IV]]) by bringing order to chaos, crushing the rebellion in [[Panthia]], and becoming a conquest paladin.
