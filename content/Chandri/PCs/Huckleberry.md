Huckleberry is a Storm Goliath that grew up in [[Coral Bay]]. His father is [[Elderberry]]. Once Huckleberry learned of [[Elderberry]]'s previous nefarious involvement, he left home to work on ships in order to get out into the world and atone for his father's deeds. One day while dragging in scrap from the reef, Huckleberry saw a mysterious air bubble surrounding an envelope attached to an anchor. When he opened the letter there was a card from the [[Deck of Many Things]], and an invitation to come aboard [[The Sea Wolf]].

After the mutiny at sea in [[Session 1]], the new crew of [[The Sea Wolf]] named Huckleberry captain, due to his sailing experience and fighting prowess.

Huckleberry seems to have developed a chronic cough, no medicine has helped as of yet.
