Newt is a goblin life domain cleric of the goddess [[Selûne]]. She is from the [[Isles of Elysia]]. She was adopted, and didn't know of her birth mother ([[Newt's Mother]]) until meeting [[Ksuch Verzeva]]. She is a currently traveling as a part of the crew of [[The Sea Wolf]], and a holder of a card from the [[Deck of Many Things]].
