Head of the School of Divination at the [[The Imperial Arcane Academy]] in [[Silverbrook]]

He is a fae-tortle chronurgy and divination wizard. He kept [[Echo Dawn Silvertail of Feather-Hoot]] safe as an egg, when he first received her from [[Elder Quillon]] hundreds of years ago, and raised her as a grand-daughter, teaching her chronurgy and the ways of Chandri.
