A brutal ruler who maintains his power through violence and destructive magic.
Possible Member of the [[Cult of the Comet]], possible holder of a card from the [[Deck of Many Things]].

One good thing to be said about him is that it seems like he doesn't want to destroy everything like the [[Cult of the Comet]] does. In fact, there have been several occasions where he has defended Chandri against outside threats like the [[Neogi]].
