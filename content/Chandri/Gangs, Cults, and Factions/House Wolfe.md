House Wolfe is an ancient house, that had impressed [High King Tenzu II], through their prowess as conquest paladins. Main influence in the military and criminal justice.

Crest: Snarling wolf head on a heater shield with a sword behind it with a crown around the hilt {The Wolf bears its fangs representing the aggression of the house, the sword and shield representing martial mastery, the crown was added to the heraldry during the rule of High King Tenzu II representing the House's chosen status as favored by the throne}

House Motto: "Fangs of the Throne" {Modern changed during the reign of High King Tenzu II when the crown was added to the crest} "Our fangs never dull" {Original House motto}

Members: Leonardo Wolfe {Head of House}, Castus Wolfe, Augustine Wolfe {Heir Apparent}, Eisenwhed Wolfe, Camille Wolfe {Castus' Wife}, Eroan Wolfe {Castus' Father and former head of house}, Aenwyn {Castus' Mother deceased}

House Wolfe was once a highly feared and powerful noble house known as the High King's Executioners but over the past 93 years it has slowly faded from relevance. While still known and respected it is now a middling noble house. Noble families that primarily focus on manufacturing or trade have risen to higher relevance in the vacuum left by House Wolfe as no major military actions have happened in the past 93 years and House Wolfe has only maintained relevance smashing upstart rebellions and large bandit gangs.
