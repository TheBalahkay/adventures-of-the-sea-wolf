
This is a wide ranging and secretive rebellion against [[High King Tenzu IV]]. It has several decentralized cells, that do not seem to know much about one another in order to keep them all safe. It may be headquartered in [[Panthia]]. 

The formerly [[Imprisoned Dwarves]] led us to one of the cells at [[Gathol~Tûm]]. 

Thanks to [[Huckleberry]]'s blunt questioning we know that they are against the enslavement of dwarves by [[High King Tenzu IV]].

They seem to be moving people/ supplies in deep underground networks that span the planet.
