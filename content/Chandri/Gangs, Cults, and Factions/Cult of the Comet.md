i.e. (Big Bad Evil Cult) 

This is the cult founded by [[Ksucha Verzeva]], (and maybe [[High King Tenzu IV]], [[Elderberry]], and [[Newt's Mother]]). Originally called "The Order" by members, the group was formed to find the cards from the [[Deck of Many Things]] and use them to bring about positive change. At some point, maybe when they found out the destructive power of bringing all the cards together, their goal was corrupted into causing the reset of all the planes. The symbol of the group is a Comet.

According to [[Gus Gus]] as of [[Session 13]]: they are primarily located within the [[Daedalian Dungeons]] and are eating lots of mushrooms to some end...
