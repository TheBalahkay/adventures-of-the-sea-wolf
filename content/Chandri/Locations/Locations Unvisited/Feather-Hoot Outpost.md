Located in [[The Emerald Vale]]. It is an old abandoned Owlin outpost, which was built hundreds of years ago. Outside the outpost, remnants of what appear to be old debris from a [[Neogi]] vessel crash lie scattered in the meadows nearby.

[[Echo Dawn Silvertail of Feather-Hoot]] was born and here and discovered by [[Elder Quillon]], who was the last remaining Owlin after the invasion. He passed away hundreds of years ago, but has left many notes and books regarding Owlin culture, the astral planes, and a random assortment of textbooks for Echo in the outpost.
