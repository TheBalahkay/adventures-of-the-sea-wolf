A large volcano and surrounding mountain range rumored in some local legends to reach down to the very molten core of Chandri, and in other local legends to be the resting place of a great sleeping [[Wyrm]] who, if woken, would destroy Chandri itself. It is considered a place of great evil and great danger. The mountains that surround Krestis are home to [[Chandri/Creatures/Remorhaz]].
