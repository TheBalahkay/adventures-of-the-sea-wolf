These fearsome beasts guard the unknown depths of the [[Daedalian Dungeons]]

They appear to be controlled by cultists of the [[Cult of the Comet]]

While truly monstrous to behold, [[Ksucha Verzeva]] appears to have no trouble with them. In fact, she seems to have made a rug out of one, whom she fondly refers to as "Manny". She also knit several members of the [[The Sea Wolf]]'s crew scarves from minotaur fur, a grandmotherly hint to stay warm as she sent them towards the snowcapped mountains surrounding [[Krestis]].
