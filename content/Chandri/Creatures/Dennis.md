The wolf that belongs to [[Lok'tar]]. Does not like Kobolds after one plucked fur from him to put into a soup aboard [[The Sea Wolf]].

Dennis, despite his fearsome aura, is generally a big softie who is good with Dwarven children and is not above being used as a pillow for a weary adventurer - provided they're in his good graces.
