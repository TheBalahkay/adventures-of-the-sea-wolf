Mama Cluck is [[Lady Cluckington]]'s mother. She is a magical bird of unknown origins. She is the partner of [[Old Man Feathers]]. 

[[Pippin Tangletoes]] and [[Old Man Feathers]] think she hails from the mythical lost continent/plane of sentient birds. Pippin beleives that she gave him the visions of her losing Lady Cluckington that lead him to stealing the egg from the [[Ashen Wing]]. She helped to train [[Lady Cluckington]] and [[Pippin Tangletoes]] before they left on their journey.

She is named in memory of [[Mama Cat]]. RIP.
